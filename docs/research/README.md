# Research reports

Sourced research behind the app's design, content and launch. Each report cites its sources inline and separates what sources say from estimates and inferences. Treat figures as of the date in each report.

| Report | Date | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Sākṣī redesign design direction](Sākṣī%20redesign%20design%20direction.md) | 2026-11-08 | How to look premium and trustworthy: ten ranked design principles, landing-page structure, interaction patterns for depth, arrival, cited answers and report status, culturally respectful visual language for Nepal, and accessibility and performance on low-end phones in sunlight. The basis of `DESIGN.md`'s working rules. |
| [Pashupati and Basantapur 3D models](Pashupati%20and%20Basantapur%203D%20models.md) | 2026-11-11 | Sourced proportions of Pashupatinath and Kathmandu Durbar Square for the 3D models (with every estimate marked), how to describe Pashupati respectfully in words, and visual guidance for the four original mascots. |
| [Sākṣī features fit and marketing kit](Sākṣī%20features%20fit%20and%20marketing%20kit.md) | 2026-11-11 | An audit of every feature and how far it is verified, what visitors to Nepal need and how well the app meets it, competing apps, and a launch marketing kit with ready-to-paste Play listing drafts. |

The working notes behind each report are kept outside git, in `research_notes/` in the working copy.
