# Changelog

What changed for people using Sākṣī. The full feature guide is [docs/FEATURES.md](docs/FEATURES.md); the engineering record, with what was and was not verified, is [docs/WORK_LOG.md](docs/WORK_LOG.md).

## Unreleased (preview APKs, 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-11)

Not yet in a Play bundle, and not yet tried on a phone.

### Guide
- **Depth control:** Glance · Story · Scholar on every curated site page, and on Explore. Your choice carries from place to place.
- **Explore** opens on a Guide card with Ask and Report beside it.
- **Photos:** place pages open on a photograph, historical plate or labelled schematic, credited on the image. 10 more sites and all three treks now have credited photographs.
- **Pashupatinath** explains who is worshipped there, in words only, with the visitor rules.
- **Bhaktapur Durbar Square** is now listed, with its entry fee.

### Website
- The site now says who owns Sākṣī: O.M.X Lab Private Limited, Bharatpur, Chitwan, in the footer, the privacy page and the page metadata.

### Map and 3D
- **Info points on the Swayambhu models:** in the 3D view, tap the (i) on the stupa or either shikhara to read what you are looking at. Text by OMX Lab.
- **Search any place:** a search bar across the top of the full map finds our sites and listed places, and any named place in Nepal from OpenStreetMap (a tole, a street, a hotel); the map flies there.
- **Tidier map controls on phones:** back, search and map type across the top; follow, walking view and the demo walk in one stack on the right with Quests and the guide. Quest memories moved into the Quests sheet. The credits fold into an (i).
- **The demo walk keeps to the streets:** each leg follows the mapped footpath instead of a straight line through houses; inside palace courtyards, where no path is mapped, it crosses directly. Steadier steps.
- **Map colours from satellite views:** warm grey ground, deeper greens, mostly flat grey concrete roofs over brick, cream and pastel walls.
- **The Tīrtha map panel shows the real 3D monuments:** it opens tilted at the modelled or scanned monument nearest you, instead of a flat plan with boxes.
- **Text size:** app text follows the phone's text size only up to a limit, and the map no longer scales its credits with it.
- **Updates in the app:** a new version downloads in the background and a bar offers a one-tap restart. When a new APK is needed, the bar offers its download.
- **Detailed monuments on the map, modelled from photos and OpenStreetMap:** Swayambhu (the stupa with its eyes, shrines and prayer wheels, the Pratappur and Anantapur shikharas and the great vajra), Maya Devi Temple and the World Peace Pagoda in Lumbini, and the Durbar Squares: Nyatapola and Bhairavnath in Bhaktapur; Kasthamandap, Maju Dega, Narayan, Trailokya Mohan and Jagannath in Kathmandu; Hari Shankar in Patan. 18 in all, each at its mapped footprint, credited as modelled, not scanned. Heights are by eye.
- **3D town:** at street zoom, 3D mode draws houses with windows and roofs instead of boxes.
- **Walking view:** the camera walks behind your figure at street level through the 3D town, with the sky ahead.
- **Real scans in the 3D map, and only real ones:** Yetkha Bahal's stupa, Keshar Mahal with the Garden of Dreams, Tusha Hiti and the Nag Bahal stone tap in Patan, and Bagdwar in Shivapuri stand on the map as real captures. Our schematic models now appear only in the 3D viewer.
- **Real 3D scans on the map:** a 3D badge marks 22 real scans, from the whole Swayambhu hill to Kal Bhairav and Mustang's Tsarang; tap to open the scan.
- **A real walking figure:** a scanned person walks with you on the map, in step with your speed, and stops when you stop.
- **Monuments on the map:** in 3D mode, close in, our 3D models of Kathmandu Durbar Square, Patan, Bhaktapur, Boudhanath, Pashupatinath, Changu Narayan, Pokhara and Lumbini stand at their sites.
- **New 3D models:** a detailed Pashupatinath (courtyard, Nandi, shrines and river), the Basantapur tower with Lohan Chowk, Taleju, Kasthamandap and Maju Dega. 13 models in all. Overall heights are sourced; other sizes are estimates, and each model says so.

### In-depth guides
- **Pashupatinath, Boudhanath and Swayambhunath:** each has a full guide.
  - A cover photograph, quick facts, and the story at your chosen depth.
  - A photo gallery and a walk whose stops open on the map.
  - What to know before going, and the entry fee.
  - Find them under "In depth" on Explore.

### Your record
- **Your record:** one screen with the places you have visited, your photos, and where each of your reports has got to. From Explore or Settings.

### Ask and Report
- **Ask about places:** entry fees, visitor rules, photography and history for the curated sites and listed places, quoted from the site page with its source. Works for questions in Nepali that use the place's Nepali name.
- **Ask page:** suggested questions and your recent questions.
- **Ask** shows its two promises before the first question.
- **Report** opens on a card that explains the flow. Each report now shows a dated timeline of what the site has done, kept for offline viewing.

### Look and feel
- **Sunlight theme:** high contrast for reading outdoors (Settings → Appearance).
- **Four original characters:** a guide, a porter, a temple macaque and a woodcarver. No deity is drawn as a character.

### Website
- **Nepal in 3D shows only real scans and the detailed models from the map**, no schematic ones, each marked "Real scan" or "Modelled by Sākṣī" with its source.
- **Redesign:** a new hero, feature grid, "one place, three verbs", "where the answers come from", and an institutions band.
- **Fix:** the explorer map draws again.
