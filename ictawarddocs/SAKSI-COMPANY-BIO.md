# Sakshi

Company Bio

*Turning every visit to a heritage site into evidence worth trusting.*

## About Sakshi

Sakshi is a heritage conservation technology project based in Nepal. Its free mobile application helps visitors to heritage sites capture dated, precisely positioned photographs and file condition reports that heritage authorities can use to monitor these places between formal inspections.

Heritage sites in Nepal are visited by millions of people every year, yet formally inspected by experts only once every few years. In that gap, real damage goes unrecorded: a crack widens, a water spout runs dry, and nobody writes it down. Sakshi closes that gap by turning the attention visitors already bring into evidence a conservation officer can trust, at no cost to the visitor and no cost to the institution collecting it.

Sakshi currently covers 15 heritage sites across Lumbini and the Kathmandu Valley. It was conceived and built independently by Team Everest, four students from Chitwan, Nepal, and won first place at the LumbiniX 2026 national hackathon.

## Key Services and Offerings

- Guided heritage site visits, explaining a monument's history and significance exactly where a visitor is standing, in Nepali or English, drawn only from real, cited historical sources.
- Fixed point photo tracking, aligning a visitor's phone with a past photograph and allowing a capture only once the position and angle genuinely match, building a photo series that can be honestly compared over time.
- Condition reporting, letting visitors flag visible damage or change, such as a new crack or water that has stopped flowing, in under a minute, with a photo attached.
- A custodian dashboard, where heritage site caretakers and authorities can acknowledge, track, and resolve every report, closing the loop between what a visitor notices and what gets fixed.
- Source grounded question answering, a built in guide that answers questions about a site's history or Buddhist teaching using only real, cited sources, and says so plainly when it cannot find an answer, rather than guessing.
- Bilingual, offline first design, with all content available in Nepali and English, and full functionality with no internet connection, saving records on the phone until a connection is available.
