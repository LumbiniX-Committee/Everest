# Changelog

What changed for people using Sākṣī. The full feature guide is [docs/FEATURES.md](docs/FEATURES.md); the engineering record, with what was and was not verified, is [docs/WORK_LOG.md](docs/WORK_LOG.md).

## Unreleased (preview APKs, 2026-11-08 to 2026-11-11)

Not yet in a Play bundle, and not yet tried on a phone.

### Guide
- **Depth control:** Glance · Story · Scholar on every curated site page, and on Explore. Your choice carries from place to place.
- **Explore** opens on a Guide card with Ask and Report beside it.
- **Photos:** place pages open on a photograph, historical plate or labelled schematic, credited on the image. 10 more sites and all three treks now have credited photographs.
- **Pashupatinath** explains who is worshipped there, in words only, with the visitor rules.
- **Bhaktapur Durbar Square** is now listed, with its entry fee.

### Map and 3D
- **3D town:** at street zoom, 3D mode draws houses with windows and roofs instead of boxes.
- **New 3D models:** a detailed Pashupatinath (courtyard, Nandi, shrines and river), the Basantapur tower with Lohan Chowk, Taleju, Kasthamandap and Maju Dega. 13 models in all. Overall heights are sourced; other sizes are estimates, and each model says so.

### In-depth guides
- **Pashupatinath, Boudhanath and Swayambhunath:** each has a full guide.
  - A cover photograph, quick facts, and the story at your chosen depth.
  - A photo gallery and a walk whose stops open on the map.
  - What to know before going, and the entry fee.
  - Find them under "In depth" on Explore.

### Your record
- **Your record:** one screen with the places you have visited, your photos, and where each of your reports has got to. From Explore or Settings.

### Ask and Report
- **Ask about places:** entry fees, visitor rules, photography and history for the curated sites and listed places, quoted from the site page with its source. Works for questions in Nepali that use the place's Nepali name.
- **Ask page:** suggested questions and your recent questions.
- **Ask** shows its two promises before the first question.
- **Report** opens on a card that explains the flow. Each report now shows a dated timeline of what the site has done, kept for offline viewing.

### Look and feel
- **Sunlight theme:** high contrast for reading outdoors (Settings → Appearance).
- **Four original characters:** a guide, a porter, a temple macaque and a woodcarver. No deity is drawn as a character.

### Website
- **Redesign:** a new hero, feature grid, "one place, three verbs", "where the answers come from", and an institutions band.
- **Fix:** the explorer map draws again.

## 1.0.0 (Play bundle, 2026-11-08, `e08d04d`)

- Onboarding and plans, with no prices in store builds.
- **Map:** modes, clustering, walking directions, offline map packs.
- **Three trekking routes** with a live trail map.
- **1,086 essentials** from Overture Maps.
- **Close-up guided visits** at marked viewpoints, condition reports and offline sync.
- **Ask** with cited answers and honest refusals.
- **Privacy:** export and delete, and a privacy policy that names every outside service.
- **English and Nepali.**
